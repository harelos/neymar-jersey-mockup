Four formulas, one feeling:
fun, friends, family, not serious.

Every batch has two hidden layers. The craft layer — backdrop, lighting, model archetype, ladder of shots. And the feeling layer — what the person is doing, what emotion the camera catches, what the buyer feels when they see it. The 99 Jerseys brand isn't about clean product shots. It's about joy, friendship, family, and playing the game with a smile. That's the layer we reverse-engineer here.

How to MIX the formulas for a full catalog

Don't pick one formula. Mix them. For every product, run some slots from each formula to get the full emotional range. Buyers scroll fast — you need trust shots (Formula 01), fit shots (Formula 02), identity shots (Formula 03), AND feeling shots (Formula 04) so no matter what caught their eye, something lands.

  1. 2 shots from Formula 04 to open the PDP. This is what stops the scroll. Joy first.
  2. 2 shots from Formula 01 for the clean product-view. Trust.
  3. 1 shot from Formula 02 for the back-detail macro. Prove the print quality.
  4. 1 shot from Formula 03 for the lifestyle / off-pitch context. This isn't just for the game.
  5. 1 ghost front + 1 ghost back as the closer, for e-commerce sanity.

= 8 shots per product, 4 emotional registers covered, every kind of buyer served.
